Who we are

A space where every
child's story matters.

Tales from the Shed believes that performing arts should be for every single child — regardless of ability, background, or experience. We create a safe, joyful, and silly space where children can express themselves, build confidence, and discover their voice.

Whether your child has never set foot on a stage or has been performing since they could walk, they'll find a home here.

🏠 Based at Chickenshed Theatre, London
🤸 Fully Inclusive Every ability, every background, every child.
😄 Joyful & Fun We take the arts seriously. Not ourselves.
💪 Builds Confidence Skills that go far beyond the stage.
🎭 Real Performances Every child gets their moment in the spotlight.

What we do

Six ways we bring
stories to life.

From drama workshops to full productions — there's something for every child at every level.

🎭

Drama Workshops

Weekly sessions exploring storytelling, character, and improvisation in a fun, pressure-free environment. No experience needed.

🎵

Musical Theatre

Singing, dancing, and acting combined — learning songs and routines from beloved musicals and original productions.

💃

Movement & Dance

Expressive movement sessions that build body confidence, coordination, and the joy of performing through dance.

📖

Storytelling

Children become the authors of their own stories — developing imagination, language, and the courage to share their ideas.

🌟

Performance Shows

Every term culminates in a real performance — at Chickenshed Theatre. Every child gets a role, every family gets to cheer.

🏕️

Holiday Workshops

Intensive creative workshops during school holidays — a week of stories, songs, and silliness that children talk about for months.
