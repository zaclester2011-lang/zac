# Scene 1: paragraph stage (Zac Lester, 20 November 2026)

*Typed from Zac's handwritten page (handwritten/02-scene1-paragraph.jpg).*

Washington D.C., January 1946. Freezing, snowing, dark. Inside the headquarters, in a small, brown, boring-looking room, with frames of Byrd in Antarctica smiling on his missions. They will be real pictures. A few autographs. A large map of Antarctica across a massive table. Five chairs around the table; no one is sitting down, everyone is leaning over the map. The Navy man starts telling everyone this is where we are going to practise for the Cold War. Byrd talks about the area opposite of where the Navy commander wants them to go, at the bottom of the map. He says how he's an explorer, and "you only brought me in 'cos of my name. Me and my crew want to explore, not do army stuff. We want to find the unknown; that's our job." The Navy commander gets furious and storms off, and Byrd starts planning his own mission to explore "Beyond the Horizon."

P.S. During the whole movie, Byrd and the Navy commander have awful tension and hostility towards each other.
