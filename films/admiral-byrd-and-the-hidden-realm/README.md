# Admiral Byrd and the Hidden Realm

**A ZAC Studio film project: first draft**

> In 1947, America's greatest explorer flew past the edge of the map.
> What he found was never in the history books — because he kept his promise.

A family sci-fi adventure based on Zac's story: Admiral Richard E. Byrd plans a polar expedition, flies through the hole into a hidden realm inside the Earth, meets the aliens who live there, tours their world, and ends the story at a press conference.

## Story structure

| Act | What happens | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The Planning** | Washington, 1946. Byrd hears a mysterious signal from under the ice, the same sound he heard when something saved his life in 1934. He assembles his crew and plans the expedition. | 1–14 |
| **2A. Through the Hole** | The voyage south, a rocket-boosted carrier launch, the flight into the blank, through the Veil and down into the hidden realm. First contact with the Ilun. | 14–37 |
| **2B. The Hidden Realm** | The tour: the city of Solace, the Garden of the Lost, the Drift, the Hall of Echoes, the feast. Then the radio mistake, the Council, and Byrd's sacrifice. | 37–63 |
| **3. The Way Home** | The escape through the closing Veil, the first sunset, the Pentagon threat, and **the press conference**. | 63–87 (press conference from p. 79) |

## How to edit and rebuild the PDF

Edit `screenplay.fountain` in any text editor, or in a Fountain screenwriting app such as [Highland](https://highland2.app) or [Beat](https://www.beat-app.fi), which show the formatting as you type. To rebuild the PDF yourself:

```bash
pip install screenplain
screenplain screenplay.fountain screenplay.pdf
```

## A note on authorship

This first draft was written with Claude (an AI) from Zac's original story. Before entering it in competitions or sending it to anyone, rewrite it in your own voice: change what you don't love and add what's missing. See [`getting-it-made.md`](getting-it-made.md#step-1-make-the-script-yours) for why that matters for copyright and for submissions.
