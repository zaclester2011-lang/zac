Case Study — Oct 2026

The Baja Boys

13 activity days
3 travellers · Three generations
Route · SJD → East Cape → Loreto
39 activities evaluated · 8 selected
Zero filler days · Zero repetition

Three generations of adventurers — a 14-year-old, a 47-year-old lifelong fisherman, and a 12-year-old performer. Fifteen nights covering every landscape type in Baja California Sur. Slot canyon swims, uninhabited island camping, world-class reef snorkelling, wilderness canyons, and Sea of Cortez fishing. No resort days. No tourist traps.

19–20 Oct
Travel
Chigwell → Heathrow → Dallas → Mexico City → SJD
23-hour travel day. Dinner at Cousin's Bar-B-Q (DFW Terminal D) and El Bajío (MEX Terminal 2).
Wed 21 Oct
Day 1
Cabo Pulmo National Marine Park
Mexico's most protected reef. Oldest living coral in the Eastern Pacific. Snorkel with rays, turtles and enormous fish schools. No boat needed — walk straight in. East Cape.
SnorkelMarine Reserve
Thu 22 Oct
Day 2
Zorra & El Chorro Waterfalls
Twin waterfalls hidden in Sierra la Laguna — both on the same dirt road 1.8 miles from Santiago town square. Enter via Rancho Ecológico Sol de Mayo. Natural swimming pools, total isolation.
WaterfallSierra la Laguna
Fri 23 Oct
Day 3
Drive North — SJD to Loreto with Hidden Stops
6-hour drive broken by El Pilar viewpoint and Agua Verde — a turquoise cove at the bottom of a dramatic dirt road with fishermen on the beach and goats on the road. Arrive Loreto evening.
Drive DayAgua Verde
Sat 24 Oct
Day 4
Isla Carmen — Private Boat Charter
45 minutes from Loreto harbour to a large uninhabited island. Remote salt lagoon, sea lion colonies basking on the rocks, crystal clear turquoise water. No other boats.
Boat CharterSea LionsSea of Cortez
Sun 25 Oct
Day 5
Cañón de Mezquite
One of the most extraordinary canyons in Baja — almost nobody goes there. Mandatory deep-water swimming through slot canyon narrows. Elusive trailhead. Total isolation. The place you remember for the rest of your life.
Slot CanyonGuide Required

Day 6
Cañón del San Carlos
Full-day wilderness endurance canyon. No markers, no other visitors, genuine backcountry terrain. River-bed route deep into the Sierra la Laguna. The hardest day — and the most earned.

Isla Monserrate — 4-Night Island Camp
Boat drop onto an uninhabited volcanic island in the Sea of Cortez. Four nights camping under the stars. Sit-on-top kayaks for coastal exploration. Satellite communicator for safety. No other people. No facilities. No noise. The centrepiece of the expedition.
