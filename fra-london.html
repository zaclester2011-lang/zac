Yes. Under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, all non-domestic premises in England and Wales must have a suitable and sufficient fire risk assessment carried out by a competent person. Failure to comply can result in significant fines or prosecution.

There is no fixed legal timeframe, but your assessment must be reviewed whenever there are significant changes to your premises, occupancy, or processes. As a general guide, annual reviews are recommended for most properties.
