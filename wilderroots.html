Case study 02

Flight Intelligence

London → Bangkok · Business Class · 12 Alternative Routings

A client was quoted £25,000 for London–Bangkok business class. By identifying 12 alternative routings across five carriers, we reduced that cost to £12,500 — the same seats, the same airports, half the price.

£12,500saved on a single booking. The research took two days.
