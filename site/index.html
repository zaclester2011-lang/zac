Type anything and the destinations filter. A category word like beach or diving ranks them by how many they hold; words like hidden, empty and secluded keep only places with almost nothing mapped around them; and any other word — navagio, cenote — is matched against the names of the places in each destination. Everything is open data, and nothing is invented: every rating here is either measured on the ground or rated by Claude from stated evidence, and anything that cannot be rated is left blank rather than filled in.
