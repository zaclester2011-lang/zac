About Form & Forge

Form & Forge is an independent creative studio based in London. We partner with ambitious founders, growing brands, and category leaders who believe that exceptional design is a business advantage — not a luxury.

We keep our team intentionally small. Every project is led by a senior creative. No account managers. No junior hand-offs. Just direct access to the people doing the work.
