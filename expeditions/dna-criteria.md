# DNA Criteria — The Scoring System

The framework used to evaluate every destination and activity.

---

## Scoring

Six categories, 8 points each. **Maximum 48.**

| Category | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Turquoise Level** | Genuine turquoise water. Not blue, not green, not clear-but-grey |
| **Hidden Feeling** | Does it feel undiscovered when you are standing there |
| **Earned Arrival** | Effort required to reach it. Boat and guide-led count — they are still earned |
| **Hidden Adventures** | Is there a real adventure attached, not just a view |
| **Hidden Water Bodies** | Quality and obscurity of the water itself |
| **DNA ★** | Overall fit: hidden, earned, turquoise, active, remote, uncrowded, Jewish-friendly |

**Cut line: 40/48.** Anything below is removed.

---

## Filters (applied absolutely, no exceptions)

1. **Pure turquoise only.** Any inland water body that is not genuinely turquoise is removed — regardless of how good the adventure is. This is why Restonica, Krka, Plitvice and the Soča were cut.
2. **Must be swimmable.** Viewpoints and waterfalls you cannot get into are removed.
3. **Caves must be proper networks of light sea caves.** Single dark passages are removed.
4. **No passive or tourist activities.** If it is looking rather than doing, it is out.
5. **No duplicates.** If a place features in another activity, it does not get scored twice.

---

## Day-planning rules

- **The 11:15 rule.** All self-directed activities start at 11:15.
- **Guide-led activities:** the operator sets the time.
- **Exception:** fishing. Fishing starts early — "a man's game".
- **Crowd optimisation.** Guide-led activities are deliberately placed on weekends to absorb the busy days, leaving self-directed activities on quiet weekdays.
- **Back by 19:00 at the latest**, comfortably, with dinner manageable.
- **Flight days get no activities** — unless the arrival lands early enough to absorb a short one.

---

## A note on the scores

The scores are estimates, constructed by Zac. They are excellent for ranking options
against each other. They are not measurements — a 45 is not verifiably better than a 44,
and differences of 1–2 points are inside the noise. Do not over-optimise on them.
