# Palawan — Full Catalogue

**Status: FIRST PASS, research opened 26 September 2026**

Scored **Discovery · Colour · Water**, each /10, total /30.
**Weak-link rule: below 7 in any category is cut, however high the total.**

Old-system score: **47/48 — #2 in the world**, behind Raja Ampat only.

---

## THE SEASON PROBLEM — READ FIRST

| Season | Months | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Amihan** (NE monsoon) | **Nov – Feb** | Dry, cool, reliable. Coron best |
| **Best window** | **Mar – May** | Glass seas, peak visibility, Balabac accessible |
| **Habagat** (SW monsoon) | **Jun – Oct** | **Palawan faces the wrong way.** Rain, swell, cancelled boats |
| Typhoon risk | Jul – Nov | Palawan is largely below the main typhoon belt — its problem is habagat, not typhoons |

**Palawan is out for August 2027.** El Nido and Coron both open **west**, straight
into the southwest monsoon. This is the opposite of the Indonesian rule: in
Indonesia the southeast trades punish east-facing coasts in August, in Palawan
the southwest monsoon punishes west-facing ones in the same month.

**Balabac is the hardest cut** — March to May only. Outside that window the
Sulu Sea crossing is genuinely dangerous and boatmen refuse it.

**Cadlao Lagoon at 26 is the first 26 found outside Indonesia in any audit** —
nine points clear of Europe's ceiling in a single entry. It is the one major
Bacuit lagoon the standard A/B/C/D tours skip.

**Big Lagoon is a Colour 10** — the full-length turquoise channel between
200 m karst walls. Discovery 6: it is the single most photographed place in
the Philippines and is now entry-capped with a paddle-only rule.

**The tour system is El Nido's real problem.** Tours A, B, C and D are fixed
itineraries run by every operator simultaneously. **A private boat is the
whole difference** between Discovery 6 and Discovery 8 here, and it is not
expensive — this is the single highest-leverage decision in Palawan.

**Kayangan Lake is the postcard and Barracuda is the better swim.** Kayangan
is 70% fresh / 30% salt in a karst bowl — Colour 10, and Discovery 5 because
it is Coron's single most visited site with a built staircase.

**Barracuda Lake scores Water 10, the only one in this catalogue.** It has a
thermocline — you swim down through distinct layers and the water goes from
about 28°C to nearly 38°C. It is 40 m deep against a vertical wall. Nothing
in the Indonesia file does this.

**Twin Lagoon's hidden half is reached by ducking under a limestone arch at
low tide** or over a ladder at high — the two halves have different
temperatures where underground freshwater springs meet the sea.

**The wrecks are cut on Colour 5.** Eleven WWII Japanese ships in Coron Bay —
a world-class dive site and grey, which is not what this list is for.

**Linapacan is the headline find of this first pass.** It sits between El Nido
and Coron, is crossed by every expedition boat between them, and is almost
never stopped at properly. **It has topped more than one "clearest water in
the world" list.**

**Maosonon scores Discovery 10** — a 45-hectare island locally called Mystery
Island, with no development and no scheduled boat. That is the first
Discovery 10 awarded to any *swimmable* water body in any audit so far
(Bosnia's Rakitnica also scored 10 and was cut on temperature).

**Four Linapacan entries at 26+.** On a first pass and with no site visits,
this is the strongest single cluster outside Indonesia's top six.

**Onuk at 27 is the highest score in Palawan.** Talcum-powder sand, pink-white
sandbars running into mirror lagoons, and a colour reported as aquamarine
rather than the usual turquoise. It is privately managed by the municipal
mayor's family and numbers are limited.

**Balabac is a genuine expedition.** It is the southernmost tip of Palawan,
roughly 8 hours by van from Puerto Princesa plus a sea crossing, in sight of
Malaysian Borneo. **Season is March–May, hard.** There is a low-level
security advisory for the Sulu Sea that needs checking properly before this
goes anywhere near a plan.

**Mansalangan drops to Water 7** — it is a sandbar, and sandbars are shallow.
Same reason Elafonissi was cut in Greece; here the rest of the score carries it.

**Port Barton is El Nido fifteen years ago** — no big resorts, a
sand-street town, and boat tours at a fraction of El Nido prices. Nothing
here reaches 25, but nothing is crowded either.

**Long Beach is 14 km, the longest white-sand beach in the Philippines**, and
scores 21 — it is long rather than vivid, and the sea in front of it is open
and ordinary. **This is the Tamborasi mistake in reverse:** do not score the
superlative, score the swim.

**The Underground River is a UNESCO site, a New7Wonder of Nature, and a hard
cut.** It is an 8.2 km navigable river inside a cave — you sit in a boat
wearing a hard hat, swimming is prohibited, and it is dark. **Colour 5,
Water 5.** Exactly the Blue Grotto failure: a spectacle, not a swim.

**Honda Bay is cut on Discovery 3** — it is the standard Puerto Princesa day
tour, on every package, and the islands are small and busy.

**Ten water bodies at 26+, two at 27.** Against Europe's audited ceiling of
25 across nine countries, and Indonesia's six 28s.

**Palawan's shape: the score is concentrated in the two places nobody goes.**
Balabac and Linapacan hold ten of the top thirteen entries between them. El
Nido and Coron — the famous halves — top out at 25 and 26, held down by
Discovery, not by water.

## THE VERDICT SO FAR

**Palawan is a Mar–May trip, not an August one.** That is the single fact that
matters and it is the reason it lost the August 2027 slot to Indonesia.

**If Palawan is ever run, the route is not El Nido–Coron.** It is
**Linapacan properly** (not as a transit stop) plus **Balabac** — which are at
opposite ends of a 650 km island and cannot be combined without a domestic
flight and roughly three days of overland time.

## GAPS — NOT YET RESEARCHED

- **Taytay** — Lake Manguao (Palawan's largest freshwater lake), Elephant Island,
  Isla Blanca, Apulit
- **Culion beyond Ditaytayan** — the whole west coast
- **Busuanga north coast** — Marily, Sangat, Lusong, Dimipac
- **Cuyo and Agutaya in any depth** — currently one search
- **Southern Palawan mainland** — Brooke's Point, Rizal, Bataraza falls and rivers
- **Green Island Bay / Roxas** — Modessa, Johnson, Arena
- **Calamian freshwater** — the Busuanga interior rivers
- **Araceli, Dumaran, Cagayancillo (Tubbataha's home islands)**
- **Security advisory status for Balabac and the Sulu Sea** — must be checked
  properly, not assumed
- **Whether Linapacan has any accommodation** beyond a handful of beach camps
