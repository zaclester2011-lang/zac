## Logistics

**Sardinia → Corsica crossing.** Two options:
1. **Car ferry** Santa Teresa Gallura → Bonifacio, ~1 hour, runs year-round. Car goes on board.
2. **Two separate car hires + foot-passenger ferry.** One-way hire Olbia → Palau was quoted at
   **€3,400** which is why this was considered. Two separate hires is likely cheaper.

**Flying between the islands was investigated and is probably not viable** — there is no
reliable direct Olbia → Figari or Olbia → Bastia service, especially in late October.
Routing via Rome would consume the whole day. **The ferry is almost certainly the answer.**

**Bases:** Cala Gonone (4 nights) · Palau (3) · Bonifacio (1) · Porto/Piana (2) · Saint-Florent (1)

## The honest weakness

**October is Sardinia and Corsica's weak month.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Sea temp | 20–22°C — genuinely swimmable, warmer than UK in August |
| Air temp | 18–22°C day, 12–14°C evening |
| **Freshwater** | **12–15°C. Gorges and river pools are a quick dip, not a swim** |
| Daylight | Sunset ~17:00 after the clocks change 25 Oct. Under 6 hours from an 11:15 start |
| Rain | **October is the wettest month in Corsica. Expect 1–2 washout days in 11** |
| Operators | Half shut by mid-October — the biggest practical risk |

The trip is roughly 70% sea, 30% freshwater. The sea majority works. The freshwater
component is compromised — treat the gorges as quick-dip stops rather than swim days.

**Also flagged:** possible repetition. Five activities (seven days) share the same
boat → turquoise → swim → back format. La Maddalena at 3 days is the main culprit.
Adding a gorge day (Spelunca or Fango) helps break this up.
