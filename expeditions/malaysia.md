## ⚠ THE SECURITY FLAG — READ BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

**Malaysia's best water is in the one part of Malaysia the UK advises against
travelling to.**

Semporna, Sipadan, Mabul, Kapalai, Mataking and the whole Tun Sakaran /
Sulu Sea coast sit inside the **eastern Sabah ESSZONE**. The area carries a
**standing kidnap-for-ransom threat from groups operating out of the southern
Philippines**, a **nightly sea curfew** enforced by the Malaysian navy, and a
**UK FCDO advisory against all but essential travel** to the eastern Sabah
coastal districts.

**This is not a theoretical flag.** Resorts on these islands have been
attacked and guests taken. The curfew is why island-hopping boats must be
back before dark.

**Verdict: every Semporna entry below is recorded at its real score and
treated as unavailable.** Malaysia's case has to stand on what is left —
and what is left is much weaker.

**This must be re-checked against current FCDO advice before any decision;
advisories move. But the scores below assume it stands.**

scores 9 and why it is a diving destination rather than a swimming one.

**Ulu Legong in Baling is the one genuine find** — a multi-tiered waterfall
with natural pools in quiet jungle, **turquoise water over rocky layers**,
reached by a **2–3 hour trek through dense greenery**. That is a real
Discovery 9 and the only turquoise freshwater located in Malaysia.

**Maliau Basin scores Discovery 10** — Sabah's "Lost World", a 390 km²
basin ringed by escarpment, permit-only, with no road to most of it. **Cut
to 24 by Colour 7**: the Maliau river is tea-coloured from peat tannins, like
almost all Bornean rivers. **Danum Valley's Segama is a Colour 5 cut** for
the same reason.

**The Selangor waterfalls near Kuala Lumpur — Kanching, Tekala, Chiling —
are weekend picnic sites**, 10 minutes from a car park, with shallow
streams. Discovery 3–5. They dominate every "best waterfalls in Malaysia"
list and none of them belong on this one.

## THE MALAYSIA VERDICT

**Without Semporna, Malaysia's best water body is a 24.**

| Score | Available in August, security-clear |
|---|---|
| **24** | Ulu Legong (Baling) · Maliau Falls (Sabah) |
| **23** | Pulau Kapas · Lang Tengah |
| **22** | Perhentian Kecil · Tioman · Rawa · Sibu · Jelawang |

**That is Europe's ceiling, at Indonesia's flight price.**

**The structural problem is Borneo's peat.** Malaysia has world-class
rainforest and world-class Discovery scores — Maliau 10, Danum 8 — and its
rivers run tea-brown because they drain peat swamp. Colour 5–7. The one
exception found, Ulu Legong, is on the peninsula over limestone.

**What Malaysia does have that Indonesia does not** is the jungle-plus-reef
combination in a single short trip: Danum Valley to Semporna is a few hours
by road. That was the original 43/48 case for Sabah. **The security advisory
removes exactly that combination**, because it removes the reef half.
