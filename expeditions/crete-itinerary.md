### Why it is ordered this way

**The 13:00 arrival buys Seitan Limania for nothing.** Chania airport and the
beach are both on Akrotiri, so you go from the hire desk to the water, not out
from the hotel later in the week.

**The charter goes Tuesday with Saturday as the retry.** It is not a season
question — every operator says *"April to October, when the sea conditions
allow"*, so **31 October is inside its window.** That puts four days of slack
behind the most fragile entry in the plan. **Domata has no usable land route:**
the E4 there is graded Δύσκολη, climbs to ~700 m, is called one of the hardest
sections in Crete, and its own recommended season is April–September.

**Gavdos goes Thursday–Friday** because the Chora Sfakion sailings are Thu–Sun,
and that keeps Saturday free for the retry.

**The eastward drift on 1–2 November** turns the two post-ferry days into
Preveli and Agiofarago while delivering you to Heraklion.

## ON THE GROUND

**Taxis:** no ride-hailing app covers western Crete — **no Uber, no FREENOW,
no Bolt.** **Chania radio dispatch: Taxi 18300.** FREENOW works in Heraklion
only, useful on the last morning.

**Apps:** **Windy** *(check it the night before every boat day — it is what the
boatmen are looking at)* · **Poseidon System, poseidon.hcmr.gr** *(the official
marine forecast; wave height is what cancels a sailing)* · **meteo.gr** ·
**WhatsApp and Viber** *(a Greek boatman in October answers Viber, not email)* ·
**Google Maps with Crete downloaded offline** *(signal drops on the south coast
and in the gorges)*.

**Weather watch, specific to these entries:** **Domata, Trypiti, Sentoni and
Karoumes are all gorge mouths.** **After heavy rain, runoff can cloud the water
at the outflow — so check the rain forecast, not just the wind.** A wet 48
hours before the charter could spoil three of them at once.

**Elafonissi rules** *(1 km from Kedrodasos)*: **park 500–600 m out, stay
outside the fenced vegetation, and taking sand is illegal under Law 1650/86.**
The pink is only at the waterline, and it is fading because people take it.

## 🔎 SENTONI VERIFIED IN GREEK — 25 → 24

**Asked directly: is Sentoni turquoise? No source says so.** Sentoni was the
tenth entry carrying an unverified score, and like the nine before it, it fell
when read in Greek.

| Source | What it actually says |
|---|---|
| Elizabeth Estate Agency | *«Η όμορφη παραλία Σεντόνι διαθέτει **βότσαλο** και **βαθιά καταγάλανα** νερά»* — pebble, and **deep, intensely blue** water |
| thisiscrete.gr | *«**βαθιά, κρυστάλλινα** νερά του Λιβυκού Πελάγους»*, below *«απόκρημνους βράχους και ψηλά πρανή»* — deep, crystalline; sheer rock and high scree slopes |
| cretanbeaches.gr | Pebble; **the neighbouring Trypiti's bottom is rocky** |

**The word is καταγάλανα, not τιρκουάζ.** Greek has a specific word for
turquoise water and uses it freely — Cape Tripiti on Gavdos got its 25 on
exactly that word. **Nobody applies it to Sentoni.** What they apply is
*βαθιά* — deep — three times over.

**That is the mechanism, not a quibble.** Turquoise is shallow water over pale
rock. Sentoni is **deep water over pebble and rock, under sheer cliffs** — the
same profile that took Delfinia from 26 to 21 and Sougia's Trypiti from 26 to
25. Deep clear water off a pebble shore reads navy and bottle-green, which is
beautiful and is not what this trip is scored on.

**Colour 8 → 7. 9·7·8 = 24.** It survives the weak-link rule by exactly one
point.

## ↩️ DOMATA RESTORED TO 26 — the murk is seasonal and I cut it out of season

**Zac was right.** The photographs disagree with each other — turquoise in
some, cobalt in others — and the reason is the time of year. **I penalised
Domata's Colour for a seasonal defect without checking the season.**

### The inconsistency was mine

Earlier in this audit, **Discovery scores penalised for crowding were all
re-scored upward**, because crowding is seasonal and late October empties out.
Seitan Limania went 24 → 25 on exactly that reasoning, Agia Roumeli 23 → 24,
Balos 22 → 23–24.

**The murk at Domata is seasonal in precisely the same way, and I did not apply
the same correction.** One filter, applied to one category and not the other.

### Why late October is the best case, not the worst

The murk is not dirt and not sediment. Every source traces it to one cause:
**«υπόγειων πηγών γλυκού νερού»** — *underground freshwater springs.* It is a
freshwater lens hazing the saltwater.

**Crete's rain falls between roughly October and April. May to September is
effectively rainless.** Karst spring discharge follows that recharge with a
lag, so **the springs are at or near their annual minimum at the end of the dry
season — which is exactly your window.** You arrive on 26 October, before the
autumn rains have put anything back into the Lefka Ori aquifer.

**The weakest freshwater plume of the year.** *(Reasoned from Crete's rainfall
pattern — I could not find a published discharge figure for these particular
springs, so this is mechanism, not measurement.)*

### The murk is also one source, repeated

**Two sites mention murk. Four put clear blue water in the headline.** I had
built a cut on the minority reading of one line.

### Restored

**Colour 6 → 8. Water 7 → 8. `10·8·8 = 26`.**

**Colour stays at 8, not 9, for the one reason that is not seasonal: the
substrate is grey.** *Fine pebbles in tones of grey*, *grey sand.* Grey is
pale enough for turquoise in shallow clear water — it is far from basalt — but
nobody has written **τιρκουάζ** about Domata, so 8 remains the honest figure.

*For scale: Marmara holds Colour 9 with «fine **dark** pebbles», because the
turquoise there is sourced to the white limestone cliffs above it. Domata sits
under the same Lefka Ori limestone. **8 may be the conservative call.***

> ### Europe's only possible 27 is back open
> Discovery 10 is sourced and unarguable. Water 8 is standard for the Libyan
> Sea in late October. **Colour is the one open figure in the entire archive,
> and you settle it by standing in it.**

### What this does NOT rescue

**Potamos stays cut.** Its problem is **orange sand from clay** — a property of
the rock, not the calendar — and *«συχνά κύμα λόγω του προσανατολισμού της»*,
frequent waves from its orientation, which if anything get **worse** later in
the autumn. Nothing seasonal to credit.

**Preveli stays at 24.** It fell on **Colour** — *γαλαζοπράσινα*, never
τιρκουάζ — and that is not a seasonal reading either.

## 🔴 RED BEACH / Κόκκινη Άμμος, Matala — 23 → 21

**The dark-substrate rule, stated out loud by the source itself.**

> *«Χαρακτηριστικό της… είναι η αφράτη **κεραμιδοκόκκινη άμμος** που προέρχεται
> από τα κοκκινωπά εδάφη της περιοχής»*
>
> *«Τα χρώματα της θάλασσας, **λόγω της άμμου**, αποκτούν ένα υπέροχο
> **πρασινογάλαζο** χρώμα»*

**Terracotta-red sand from the region's red soils — and the sea takes its
green-blue colour BECAUSE OF that sand.** The causal chain written down:
*substrate → water colour.*

This is the rule that cut **Aspes** (*the black beach of the Asterousia*),
**Pyrgos** and **Potamos** on Gavdos, and **Seixal** on Madeira. Everywhere
else I inferred it. **Here the guide states it.** Red sand gives
*πρασινογάλαζο* — green-blue, olive-tinged. Genuinely beautiful. **Not
turquoise, and it cannot be**, because turquoise needs a pale floor.

### The other two scores

**Discovery 7.** It is **800 m from Matala**, one of the most famous beaches in
Crete. 15–25 min over the Kastri hill with some scrambling and a steep final
descent — a real little walk — **but there are umbrellas and a canteen on the
sand**, a boat runs from Matala harbour, and protothema lists it among
*«τις έξι καλύτερες παραλίες γυμνιστών στον κόσμο»* — **the six best nudist
beaches in the world.** Nothing on that list is hidden.

**Water 7.** *«ευάλωτη στους **δυτικούς ανέμους** που πνέουν **συχνά** στην
περιοχή»* — exposed to the west winds, which blow often. Wind-driven chop over
fine red sand is the sediment problem again.

**`7·7·7 = 21.`** It clears the weak-link rule with nothing to spare.

### Consequence

**Monday 2 November is Agiofarago 24, outright.** Red Beach was carried in the
itinerary as the alternative on that day; at 21 against 24 it is not a
contender. **A 3 km gorge walk ending at the Goumenospilio cave beats a
famous nudist beach with a canteen, by three points.**

*Keep Red Beach as a 20-minute curiosity if you are standing in Matala anyway —
the colour is worth seeing precisely because it is the wrong colour.*
