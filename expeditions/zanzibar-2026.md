## TIDES AND THE MOON

**The full moon is Monday 26 October 2026**, not the 24th. Kendwa Rocks schedules
its party on the nearest Saturday, so the 24th is plausible — **but confirm it,
because Sat 31 is the other candidate and the whole trip is built on this night.**

Full moon on the 26th means **spring tides 26–29 Oct**, easing toward **neap by
1–2 Nov**. Biggest range on Wed 28; smallest on Sun 1.

| Date | Day | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Wed 28** | Mangrove lagoon | **HIGH** water 13:00–17:00. At low tide it is bare sand |
| **Fri 30** | Nakupenda | **LOW** water. +4 from full moon — a stronger low than Sunday would give |
| **Sat 31** | Mtende | **UNRESOLVED — see below** |
| **Sun 1** | Pungume | **None.** The only tide-blind stop, so it takes the neap day |

### Mtende — sources contradict each other

- One source: the sheltered lagoon forms at **low** water; *"swimming is not
  advised during high tide when currents strengthen and the lagoon disappears"*
- Another: *"at high tide, the cove's calm waters invite safe swimming"*, and at
  low tide the sea *"retreats hundreds of meters"*

Both agree the range is extreme and timing decides the day. **Ask in Jambiani
that morning** and be ready to swap Kuza and Mtende around. **Reef shoes either
way** — coral and sea urchins.

## TUMBATU — how to be there well

Access needs an approved guide who seeks the elders' blessing in advance. Dress
before arriving: long trousers, sleeves. **Always ask before photographing.**
Don't hand money or sweets to children — it makes the next visitor's welcome
worse. Give anything through the guide to the elders.

**Learn Shikamoo** (*shee-kah-MOH*), the respectful greeting to an elder; the
reply is *Marahaba*. Say it to each elder in turn. **Assalamu alaikum** also
works well. Ask the guide on the 30-minute crossing to teach a few words of
**Kitumbatu** — the island's own dialect, which mainland Zanzibaris don't use.

**Fishing is the real bridge.** The Watumbatu are known across Zanzibar as its
finest sailors and fishermen. Ask about the boats.

Change before Kendwa. Arrive at Tumbatu as guests of a religious village.

## SCORING

Three categories out of 10 — **Discovery, Colour (turquoise or emerald), Water
(amount and swimmability)** — for 30 total. A day takes its best water body's
score where one genuinely carries it, otherwise the average.

**Weak-link rule:** anything below 7 in any category is cut regardless of total.

**Confidence.** Ten of the thirteen were verified against multiple independent
sources in September 2026. The optional Menai islet rests on a Wikipedia stub and
one sentence about the southern islets being undisturbed — it is marked
provisional and left optional for that reason.
