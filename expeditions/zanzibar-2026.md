### The big saving: book boats directly at the jetty

Hotel and agency bookings carry 40–50% markups. Arranging a dhow with the owner
in person at Fumba, Mkokotoni or Wesha is dramatically cheaper for the same boat.

| Booked through | Private Fumba dhow |
|---|---|
| Hotel or agency | £150–250 |
| **Direct at the jetty** | **£80–140** |

**Across five boat days that's £300–400.** The driver will know who to talk to —
which is the strongest argument for a local driver over a hire car.

### Transport

**No Uber in Zanzibar, and never has been.** Bolt launched September 2025 but
covers **Stone Town and the airport only** — cars are rare, mostly tuk-tuks and
boda bodas. No coverage at Fumba, Mkokotoni, Jambiani, Michamvi or anywhere on Pemba.

| Day | Transport |
|---|---|
| **Sun 1** | **None** — walk to the harbour. Don't book a driver |
| **Thu 29** | **Bolt** — Mbweni is 15 min, inside coverage. Saves £26–34 |
| All other days | **Driver** — £45–55/day |
| **Mon 2, 03:50** | **PRE-BOOK a taxi the day before.** Bolt will have nothing at that hour |

## THE FUMBA DAY — remember

**Pungume 3h + Vundwe 2h + ONE islet 45 min.** Four islands does not fit.

Pungume is **45 minutes south**, at the far end of Menai Bay — that transit is why
the day can't hold more. Both Pungume and Vundwe score 27; the alternatives
(Kwale 25, Miwi 24, Niamembe 24, Pamunda ~23, Sume ~23) are all lower.

**Third stop: ask the skipper on the day** which islet has the best water that week.
Kwale is the fallback but it's the Safari Blue hub and therefore the busiest.

## SCORING

Three categories out of 10 — **Discovery, Colour (turquoise or emerald), Water
(amount and swimmability)** — for 30 total. A day takes its best water body's
score where one genuinely carries it, otherwise the average.

**Weak-link rule:** anything below 7 in any category is cut regardless of total.
That removed Mnemba (discovery 4), whale sharks (discovery 4, colour 4) and every
tidal east-coast beach (water 4).

**Confidence varies.** Misali, Vumawimbi, Kuza, Chumbe, Bawe, Nakupenda, Tumbatu,
Mtende, Michamvi Kae and the Kwale lagoon were researched properly.
**Pungume, Vundwe, Niamembe and Pamunda were not** — those rest on Wikipedia
stubs, and Pungume and Vundwe carry a whole day at 27.

Research moved six scores during planning: Blue Lagoon +3, Mtende down then up,
Latham removed entirely, Michamvi +2, Pongwe −5, the Kwale lagoon +2. The
unverified entries remain the biggest risk in this plan.
