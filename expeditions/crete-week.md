## ⏰ THE THREE DEADLINES

**1 · 31 OCTOBER — the ANENDYK ferry stops.** After that: no Loutro, no Glyka
Nera, no Marmara boat, no Sougia charter. **Everything on water happens
Monday 26 to Saturday 31.**

**2 · 25 OCTOBER — the clocks change your first night. Sunset ~17:20.**
Gorge walks start early; boats must be off the water by about 16:30.

**3 · TARMAC ONLY.** Cretan rental insurance **excludes unpaved roads
entirely — including full-damage waivers.** Some firms fit GPS trackers;
penalties run to €300 plus the towing bill, and **a 4×4 does not restore
cover** because the exclusion is written against the surface, not the vehicle.
**Where the road turns to dirt, that is the car park.** Nothing on this list
needs more.

## 🗓 THE SIX DAYS — two fixed, four flexible

### MUST DO — these are the trip

**① THE SOUGIA CHARTER → Domata 26 + Trypiti 25 + Pefki 25**
Three water bodies in one morning, one of them the best in Europe on these
dates. **Book with a date range and pick the morning off the forecast.**
**Domata has no usable land route** — the E4 there is graded Δύσκολη, climbs
to ~700 m, is called one of the hardest sections in Crete, and its own
recommended season is April–September. The Kladou gorge behind it is harder
still. **Boat or nothing.**

**② THE ARADENA GORGE → Marmara 26**
**Drive to Chora Sfakion, taxi up to Aradena, walk down, swim, boat to
Loutro, ferry back to Sfakion — the car is waiting.** Do not drive to Anopoli
and walk down, or the car ends up at the wrong end. **Early start.**

### THEN PICK FROM

| | |
|---|---|
| **Glyka Nera + Loutro** | One easy day. Ferry from Sfakion, 30-minute coastal walk. **Loutro 24 is a car-free village with no road to anywhere** |
| **Kedrodasos + Elafonissi** | **One day, both together — they are 1 km apart.** Kedrodasos is the 25: walk in from the end of the tarmac. **Then drive the kilometre to Elafonissi** — ✗ cut on Water 6 *(ankle-to-knee deep)*, but **pink at the waterline and near-empty in late October.** Natura 2000: **park 500–600 m out, stay outside the fenced vegetation, and taking sand is illegal under Law 1650/86** |
| **Seitan Limania** | **45 min. Half a day.** The flexible one — slots anywhere |
| **Imbros gorge** | **1h20. 8 km downhill, 2–3 hrs**, ends near Sfakion. **The sensible alternative to Samaria** *(16 km one way, and it normally closes at the end of October)* |
| **Therisso gorge + Chania old town** | **30 min and 15 min.** You drive through the gorge. A half day that costs nothing |
| **The hotel beach** | **50 m away. Take at least one day doing nothing** |

**It is in Lasithi, not Chania** — a small seaside settlement near Skinias,

*"at the exit of the Skinias gorge, **secluded**, with crystal-clear waters."*
**23.**

## TWO STRUCTURAL FINDINGS

**1 · Why Gavdos has four 25s and no 26.** Its sand is **golden, reddish or
brown-terracotta — never white.** Blue-green water over warm-coloured sand
caps at **Colour 8**. **Marmara earns Colour 9 from pale marble; Gavdos has no
pale rock anywhere on it.** Every Gavdos beach scores **Discovery 9** and
**Colour 8** — it is access-rich and colour-poor, the exact inverse of
Crete's south coast.

**2 · Sediment is the most consistent colour-killer in this archive.** Clay at
Stavrolimni and at Argilos · churned water at Martsalo · peat in Borneo · the
Everglades' tannins · river silt at the Acheloos, the Nestos and the Evros
deltas. **Across three continents, suspended sediment has cut more entries
than temperature.**

## WHAT REMAINS GENUINELY UNSCORABLE

**Gavdos' west-coast cliff beaches and Diana** · **Gavdopoula** *(migratory

certainly landing-restricted, the Gioura and Piperi pattern)* ·
**Anidri and Gialiskari** *(three searches, nothing)* · **Fetifes' water** ·
**the two Ilingas coves** *(one source)*.
