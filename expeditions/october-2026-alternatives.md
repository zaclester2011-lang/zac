## Package market — verified

| Region | Package market | Realistic saving |
|---|---|---|
| Yucatán | **Strong** — TUI, Virgin, Thomas Cook | £600–900 |
| Zanzibar | **Strong** — established UK destination | £1,200–2,000 |
| St Vincent | Weak — few hotel contracts | £200–400 |
| Belize | **None** — specialist operators only | £0 |
| Madagascar | **None** — bespoke operators only | £0 |

**Three reasons not to buy a tour-operator package:**
1. Packages pin you to one resort. Every good itinerary here is multi-base.
2. All-inclusive is wasted — you are out from 11:15 until evening on 10 of 11 days.
3. Packages sell in 7s and 14s. This trip is 11 nights.

**Use a dynamic package instead** (Expedia, lastminute, Travel Republic): bundles
flight + multiple hotels in one ATOL-protected booking, keeps the multi-base structure,
captures most of the 10–25% saving.
