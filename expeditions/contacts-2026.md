**Save these to your phone before you fly. There is no ride-hailing app
coverage in western Crete or on Milos — everything here is a phone call,

## BALOS — getting there on foot (decision: drive + transfer + walk)

Zac's call: drive to the nearest/cheapest parking, then take a vehicle to the
nearest point, then walk in. Researched 24 Oct 2026.

### Taxis will NOT drive the track
The 8 km dirt road from Kalyviani is as closed to them as it is to our hire
cars. A Tripadvisor answer: "no buses or taxis are going on the last 7-8 km of
dirt road." The one Chania taxi firm that publishes a Balos page describes the
dirt road only as something the reader drives themselves, and quotes no price
for it. A cruise port guide adds that Kissamos has **no taxi rank** and limited
availability, and advises pre-booking any return.

|---|---|---|

### The vehicle that DOES do it — 4x4 off-road bus
A licensed commercial operator runs the track daily from Kissamos town.

- **Strata Tours** — "4x4 Low Cost Bus Transfer to Balos"
  - stratatours.com/tour/4x4-low-cost-bus-transfer-to-balos/
  - **EUR 27 pp** direct (EUR 41 via GetYourGuide — book direct)
  - **Five = EUR 135**
  - Departs Kissamos **08:00** or **09:30**
  - 5–5.5 h round trip: **40 min on dirt road + 20 min walk down**
  - **EUR 1** lagoon entrance paid separately
  - Hire cars stay in Kissamos town

So the shape is: **drive to Kissamos -> 4x4 bus over the track -> walk down.**
Legal vehicle, no insurance problem, still arrive on foot.

### UNCONFIRMED — one email to settle
1. **Does it run Sat 31 October?** No source gives a season end date. The boat
   cruise's last sailing was 31 Oct, so this coast is right at the edge.
2. Is there a barrier at the car park once the operators stop?

### Fallback if the season is over
Option 3 — walk the whole peninsula from Kalyviani: **8–10 km, ~2.5–3 h each
way**, plus the 20–40 min descent. A real day, and it would earn Balos
**Discovery 8**.

### Car park facts
End of the 8 km gravel road. **EUR 3 per car** (not needed if we don't drive it).
Car park to sand: **~2 km, 30–40 min down**, uphill on the way back (~20 min by
the taxi firm's reckoning, 30–40 by the Kalyviani guide's).

### WIND — the thing that actually decides Balos (Zac, 24 Oct)
Zac: "balos is very windy." Correct, and it bears on the colour, not just comfort.

e-meteo.gr (a weather site, not a travel blog): **the lagoon opens west and
north and is worst affected by north-west wind** — the prevailing direction on
that coast. Supporting:

- Wind **builds through the afternoon**; mornings are reliably calmest.
- The **isthmus by the canteen** funnels it worst. The inner lagoon is shallow
  and reported "clear water, no waves" on good days.
- Strong days: blowing sand, strong shore currents.
- One boat-rental operator calls Balos "exposed and frequently rough water" and
  puts it **off-limits to their hire boats**.

**Why it hits the score.** Balos' **Colour 9** came entirely from flat-water
photos — mirror-calm shallows over white sand. Chop breaks the surface and
stirs the sand, and the cyan goes grey-green. Same mechanism as the cloud rule,
arriving by a different route. **A windy Balos is not a 26.**

### CONSEQUENCE: Balos comes off Saturday
Dropping the cruise removed the week's only hard deadline (the 31 Oct last
sailing). Balos is therefore **movable to any day** and should be the
**forecast-chosen day** — the flattest, clearest morning of the week, at first
light.

### And that flips drive-vs-bus

| | |
|---|---|
| 4x4 bus | fixed date, fixed 08:00/09:30 departure, fixed return. Booked for the 31st and blown out = day lost. |
| Drive ourselves | pick the morning, go at first light, leave when the wind gets up. |

**Wind flips it to driving.** Not because the track got safer, but because an
exposed NW-facing lagoon demands the freedom to choose the morning, and the bus
cannot give it. The standing rule ("where the road turns to dirt, that is the
car park") bends **once**, for a documented reason, on the one beach where
timing decides whether we see the thing at all.

Mitigations if we drive: slowest possible pace (nearly all damage is
speed-related), check whether it has rained in the preceding days, and prefer
the higher-clearance of the two hire cars. No insurance and no off-road
recovery either way — that exposure is accepted knowingly.

Zac: "its similar in chora sfakion to sougia." He was right and the section
above was wrong. Tested by sampling the sea **2 km offshore** of each beach as a
control, plus adding **Chora Sfakion** (35.2017/24.1372).

Every point in the set reads **identically** at its beach cell and 2 km out —
except Marmara:

| Morning | Marmara **beach** | Marmara **2 km offshore** |
|---|---|---|
| Mon 26 | 79% | 69% |
| Tue 27 | 88% | 97% |
| Wed 28 | **94%** | **80%** |
| Thu 29 | **87%** | **48%** |
| Fri 30 | 98% | 98% |
| Sat 31 | 100% | 99% |
| Sun 1 | 95% | 95% |
| **Mon 2** | **94%** | **25%** |

69 points apart on Mon 2 Nov. Sougia's beach cell matches its own offshore cell
**exactly** on all eight days (55/55, 91/91, 47/47, 15/15...), so Sougia is
sound and it is specifically the Marmara cell that is contaminated by the White
Mountains behind it.

### RETRACTED
- The Marmara cloud figures above.
- The claim that Marmara is "the cloudiest point every single day."  It is not.
- The implication that Wed 28's Aradena-to-Marmara day is a write-off. It is not.
  **Marmara's 27 stands untouched.**

### The eastern stretch is literally ONE weather cell
Marmara-offshore, Loutro, Glyka Nera and Chora Sfakion return the **same**
numbers, not similar ones: **69 · 97 · 80 · 48 · 98 · 99 · 95 · 25**.

### Sougia vs Chora Sfakion — the two ends, ~30 km apart
**Cloud:** similar on five of eight mornings (26, 27, 31, 1, 2). On the other
three Sougia is the clearer end — Wed 47 vs 80, **Thu 15 vs 48**, Fri 59 vs 98.

**Wind:**

| | Sougia | Chora Sfakion |
|---|---|---|
| Mon 26 | 6.0 SSW | 9.2 SW |
| Tue 27 | 4.1 ENE | 3.5 NW |
| Wed 28 | 10.5 NNE | 12.8 N |
| Thu 29 | 5.1 NE | 3.7 NNW |
| Fri 30 | 2.9 E | 1.6 NW |

Within a few knots on six of eight; the two that diverge are the lee days

### VERDICT: treat it as one region
The cloud gradient is **much smaller** than the previous section claimed — one

### METHOD RULE ADDED
A coastal grid cell under a high massif can report the mountain's cloud, not the
shore's. **Always sample a control point 2 km offshore before trusting a cloud
figure for a beach with mountains behind it** — which on this trip is most of
them.
