# CONTACTS — October 2026 trip

**Save these to your phone before you fly. There is no ride-hailing app
coverage in western Crete or on Milos — everything here is a phone call,
a WhatsApp or a Viber message.**

> **Install WhatsApp *and* Viber before you go.** The two numbers that matter
> most on this trip are a boatman in Sougia and a taxi office on Milos.
> **Neither answers an app — both answer a message.** Viber is widely used in
> Greece, often more than WhatsApp among people over 40, which is exactly who
> runs a water taxi.

## 🛥 MILOS — TAXIS

**No Uber. No app of any kind. Phone or WhatsApp only.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Milos taxi** | **+30 22870 22219** |
| **Milos taxi** | **+30 22870 21306** |

**Based at Adamas and Triovasalos.** The fleet is **very small** — one source
warns that **when a ferry arrives they are taken immediately**, leaving a long
wait. **Do not plan a ferry connection around getting a taxi.**

**Buses:** KTEL hub at Adamas. Timetables at **milosbuses.com**.
**Thin off-season**, especially evenings — one example has the last bus from
Pollonia at 20:00.

**Car hire:** agencies at **Adamas, the airport and Pollonia**. Off-season you
can usually book on the spot. **Pick up at the airport on arrival, drop at the
ADAMAS port agency — not the airport — on the ferry morning**, so you are not
relying on a taxi to reach the quay on the one day it matters.

## 🇬🇷 WEST CRETE — THE BOATS THAT MATTER

### Sougia — the Domata charter

**This is the single most important contact on the trip. It is worth a 26.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sougia Taxi Meletis** *(Meletis Nempavlakis)* | **meletistaxisougia@gmail.com**<br>**+30 694 085 9860**<br>Papaderou St, Sougia Selinou 73009 |
| **Sougia Cruises** | **soygiacruises@hotmail.com**<br>**+30 693 407 8684**<br>**+30 697 322 0472** |
| **Yannis Paterakis** — traditional wooden caïque | **+30 697 322 0472** |

**Meletis runs both the road taxi and the water taxi.** He serves **Domata,
Trypiti, Marmara, Lykos and Gavdos**, and also works **out of Agia Roumeli**,
which the daily ferry reaches. **Sougia Cruises covers Domata, Trypiti, Lissos
and Agios Antonios.**

**Three operators across two ports. If one will not go, ask the next.**

**What to ask:**
> *"A charter from Sougia to Domata and Trypiti, the morning of [date], three
> people. What does it cost, and will you go?"*

**Price anchor: Sougia → Trypiti was €40 in October 2020.** Budget €60–100 for
**the boat**, not per person. **Trypiti and Pefki are on the way, so one
charter reaches three water bodies in a morning, one of them a 26.**

## 🏝 GAVDOS — STILL TO FIND

**The biggest open question on the trip is not the ferry. It is whether
anything is open.**

208 residents at the 2021 census. **One ATM, at Karave port. One post office
and one medical clinic, both at Kastri.** Four searches, including in Greek,
found nothing about what operates in late October — and the island's economy
is a summer rooms-and-camping scene at Sarakiniko and Agios Ioannis.

**Numbers needed:** Gavdos municipality · Karave port office · any rooms at
Sarakiniko or Agios Ioannis.

**Ask plainly: are rooms and a taverna operating on the 27th and 28th?
If the answer is vague, do not go.** Wednesday is already a rest day at
Paleochora, so dropping Gavdos costs one 25 and nothing else.

**Apps that actually earn their place:** **Windy** *(check it the night before
every boat day — it is what the boatmen are looking at)* · **Poseidon System,
poseidon.hcmr.gr** *(Greece's official marine forecast — wave height is what
cancels a sailing)* · **meteo.gr** *(the forecast Greeks use)* ·
**Ferryhopper** · **Google Maps with Crete and Milos downloaded offline**
*(signal drops on the south coast and in the gorges)* · **Organic Maps** for
dirt tracks and footpaths.
