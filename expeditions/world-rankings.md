## Notes on the Med in October

Sardinia (44) drops to roughly #15 and Corsica (43) to #18 when ranked for October
specifically, because it is their weak month. Neither is hot: 19°C air, 21°C sea,
freshwater 12–15°C.

**Cyprus is the only Mediterranean answer to the temperature problem** — 28°C air,
26°C sea in late October — but it scores 5 points lower than Sardinia.
