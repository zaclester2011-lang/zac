## Why it scores 45/48

Desert meets turquoise sea. The Sea of Cortez — Cousteau's "aquarium of the world" —
with sea lion colonies, whale sharks, and beaches reached by boat or track only.
Espíritu Santo is uninhabited and protected.

**The one weakness: no freshwater.** Baja is desert. Every water body is the sea.
That creates the same repetition risk flagged on the Sardinia boat days, but worse.

## Seasonality — important

**Whale shark season in La Paz runs mid-October to April.** Late October is the
opening of the season. That is the single strongest argument for the timing.

October is also past hurricane season for Baja, which August and September are not.
