### The problem, stated plainly

**The best water on Route C is Secret Cove at 24** — eighth on the list.

That is geometry, not bad luck. California's best water is either **in the far
north** (Smith River, Canyon Creek, McCloud) or **deep in wilderness** (Sixty
Lakes, Thousand Island, Seven Teacups). No line that also takes in six cities and
Route 66 can reach any of it.

**Two corrections worth keeping:**

- **Big Pine Lakes is a 15-mile round trip with 3,000ft of climbing**, not the
  half-day I first said. **Second Lake is the vivid one — Third Lake is shallower
  and partly dries to mud flats in late summer.**
- **Lake Tahoe is NORTH of San Francisco** (39.2°N vs 37.8°N). If the trip is
  framed as "south of the first stop", Secret Cove drops out and the best
  roadside water on the route goes with it.

### And in October it is cold

Tahoe ~15°C. High alpine lakes near freezing. Seven Teacups at its lowest flow.

**California's turquoise is glacial flour — cold by definition.** There is no
mechanism here for warm turquoise water; that needs shallow sea over pale sand,
which California does not have anywhere.

> **Go in knowing this is a road trip with water bodies on it, not a water
> expedition.** You will look at more turquoise than you swim in.

## THE RV QUESTION

**An RV costs MORE than a car and motels, not less.**

| | RV | Car + motels |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle | $1,690 | $800 |
| One-way drop | ~$500 | ~$300 |
| **Mileage — 39¢/mile** | **$585** | — |
| **Fuel — 6–9 mpg** | **~$1,000** | $250 |
| Bedding & kitchen kits | **$350** | — |
| Nights | ~$300 | $1,920 |
| **Total** | **≈ $4,400 / £3,450** | **≈ $3,270 / £2,570** |

**Three traps:** mileage at **39¢/mile**, pre-estimated at **250 miles per night**
on a one-way rental — check whether an unlimited package is cheaper. Fuel economy
of **6–9 mpg**. And bedding, towels and cookware are **separate kits at $75/head
plus $125/vehicle**.

### But Alabama Hills is free

BLM land, no fee, first-come first-served, 14-day limit. **Free permit required**
(online or the Eastern Sierra Visitor Center in Lone Pine). **Designated sites
only** now. Lone Pine has a dump station and fresh water for RVs up to 50ft.

**You cannot buy that night with a motel at any price** — sleeping among the
boulders where 400 westerns were shot, with Mount Whitney behind you.

### The answer: RV for the middle only

**San Francisco and Los Angeles are the worst possible places for a 25ft box** —
no overnight street parking, campgrounds 30–60 min out.

## TIOGA PASS — THE ONE REAL RISK

**It closes on snow, not on a date.**

| | |
|---|---|
| 20-year median closing | **14 November** |
| 20-year average | 17 November |
| Longer historical median | **3 November** |
| Opens | Mid-May to July |

Late October is fine most years. **Plan B if it closes: go round the south via
Bakersfield and Highway 58 — adds about five hours.**

**Fall colour peaks mid-to-late October** at Tioga, the June Lake Loop and Bishop
Creek — so the aspens and the pass are on the same knife-edge.

## FOOTBALL — WHAT ZAC CAN ACTUALLY DO

**A tackle game is structurally impossible for a visitor.** CIF requires every
individual player to complete **10 days of practice before competing in a game**,
plus registration, a US sports physical, insurance and age/weight certification.

**Texas is stricter, not looser** — UIL requires enrolment plus residency in the
school's specific attendance zone, verified with leases and utility bills. They
rule their own players ineligible over it.

| What | Where | Bookable? |
|---|---|---|
| **1-to-1 with a vetted coach**, 60–90 min | **LA · San Diego · SF · San Jose** | **Yes** — CoachUp or Athletes Untapped, $60–100/hr |
| **Cal one-day camp**, 14–18, all abilities | Berkeley | **Yes** — register online |
| **Offense-Defense camp**, 7–17, full pads + scrimmages | Sacramento State | **Yes** — but multi-day, and summer only |
| Friday night high school game | Anywhere | **October only** — this is the spectacle |

**October gets you Friday night lights to watch. June–July gets you playing.**
They pull opposite ways.

**The only place to actually play games is home** — BAFA runs a **U16 contact
league for ages 13–15**, 26 teams in four regional divisions, four tournaments of
two games each. Eight-game season. Adult-grade helmets mandatory.

## THE HONEST SUMMARY

**As a water expedition this is a clear step down** — nothing above 24 on the
route, everything cold, and the good stuff needs overnight hikes.

**As a trip it may be the best thing on the list**, because it is the only one
that is not the same shape as the last one. Zanzibar is eight variations on
turquoise-over-coral. This is alpine lakes, then a desert of 1950s neon, then two
great cities — three completely different things in one fortnight.

**Don't score this one against Zanzibar. It's a different category.**
