The five, ranked

Every one is genuinely hot. That was the filter.

Sardinia and Corsica came off the table because late October is their weak month — 19°C air, 21°C sea, and freshwater at 12–15°C. Every option below runs 30–32°C air and 27–29°C sea for your exact dates.

Why Yucatán leads on both axes

It is the only one of the five with a direct flight from London, and the only one where you drive to activities rather than chartering a boat to reach them. Those two facts account for almost the entire cost gap — roughly £2,000 against Belize and Madagascar.

Check before booking

Cenote entry fees rose 18% in January 2026 — currently £14–26pp. Driving between towns after dark is not advised. Quintana Roo is not on the FCDO advise-against list, but hire-car insurance in Mexico is mandatory and routinely doubles the quoted daily rate.

Highest score, lowest cost, direct flight, and the only itinerary here where half the water is fresh. It wins on the criteria as written.

The value case

Two-week five-star all-inclusive Zanzibar packages advertise from under £1,500pp including flights, transfers and every meal. If a version of that can be split across Pemba and Zanzibar, this becomes the cheapest trip on the list in real terms — the £4,600 figure does not include food, and an all-inclusive would.

No hurricane season, no safety caveat, genuinely dry and hot, and Pemba is the most under-visited place on the whole shortlist.

Hurricane season

Atlantic season runs to 30 November. Late October is past the peak but not clear — roughly a one in ten chance of a storm affecting your dates in the eastern Caribbean. Most likely that means one or two days of cancelled boats rather than a cancelled trip, but the whole itinerary is boat-dependent, which makes it more exposed than the others.

The best individual site on the shortlist, wrapped in the most weather-exposed itinerary. Everything here depends on boats running.

The Glover's Reef problem

Atoll resorts sell mostly as week-long packages. Short stays do exist — listings show rooms from around $604, and Isla Marisol includes three meals daily plus the return boat from Dangriga — but many atoll lodges run fixed weekly transfer days, often Saturday only. A Tuesday-to-Thursday stay may simply not be bookable. This needs confirming directly with the resort before anything else in the itinerary is fixed, because three days depend on it.

The strongest jungle-and-reef mix on the list, and the most likely to break on a logistics detail nobody publishes.

The honest downsides

Flights are the most expensive on the list at £1,150–1,277 typical, there is no UK package market at all, and infrastructure is poor — internal transfers are slow and roads are bad. Crime risk is moderate and poverty-driven. You are paying the most for the highest single site on the list, surrounded by the most friction.

Nosy Hara is the best thing on any of these five itineraries. Everything around it is harder and more expensive than it is anywhere else.
